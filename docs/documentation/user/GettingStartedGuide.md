## Creating Your Account

### Sign Up Options

BookReview Platform offers multiple ways to create your account:

#### Email Registration

1. Click the **Sign Up** button in the top-right corner of the homepage
2. Enter your full name, email address, and create a password
3. Click the **Create Account** button
4. Check your email inbox for a verification link
5. Click the verification link to activate your account

![Registration Form](../images/placeholder-registration-form.png)

#### Social Media Login

You can also create an account or log in using your social media accounts:

1. Click the **Sign Up** button in the top-right corner
2. Choose either "Continue with Google" or "Continue with Facebook"
3. Follow the prompts to authorize BookReview Platform
4. Review and confirm the information imported from your social account

### Password Requirements

For security, your password must contain:
- At least 8 characters
- At least one uppercase letter
- At least one lowercase letter
- At least one number
- At least one special character

### Privacy Settings

By default, your profile will be public, allowing other users to see your reviews and favorite books. You can adjust your privacy settings after registration in your account settings.

## Navigating the Platform

### Main Navigation Menu

The platform includes the following main sections:

- **Home**: Your personalized dashboard with recommendations and recent activity
- **Browse Books**: Explore the catalog of books by category, popularity, or recent additions
- **My Profile**: Access your profile, reviews, and favorite books
- **Search**: Find books, authors, or other users

![Navigation Menu](../images/placeholder-navigation.png)

### Quick Access Toolbar

At the top of every page, you'll find:

- **Search Bar**: Quickly find books, authors, or users
- **Notifications**: Alerts for activity related to your reviews or profile
- **Account Menu**: Access your profile, settings, and logout option

## Finding Books

### Browse Categories

1. Click on **Browse Books** in the main navigation
2. Use the category filters on the left side to narrow your search
3. Select a genre, publication year, or rating range
4. Results will update automatically as you select filters

### Search Functionality

1. Click in the search bar at the top of any page
2. Begin typing a book title, author name, or keyword
3. Select from the dropdown suggestions or press Enter to see full results
4. Use the advanced search options to refine your query

#### Advanced Search Tips

- Use quotes for exact phrases: `"The Great Gatsby"`
- Exclude terms with a minus sign: `fantasy -young adult`
- Search for books by a specific author: `author:Tolkien`
- Filter by rating: `rating:4+`

## Reading and Writing Reviews

### Reading Reviews

1. Navigate to any book detail page
2. Scroll down to the **Reviews** section
3. Reviews are sorted by default from most to least helpful
4. Change the sort order using the dropdown menu
5. Click **Show More** to expand longer reviews

### Writing Your First Review

1. Navigate to the book you want to review
2. Click the **Write a Review** button
3. Rate the book using the 1-5 star rating system
4. Write your review in the text box (up to 5,000 characters)
5. Optionally add images by clicking **Add Images**
6. Click **Submit Review** when you're ready to publish

![Review Form](../images/placeholder-review-form.png)

### Review Guidelines

For a helpful review, consider including:
- What you liked or disliked about the book
- Who would enjoy this book
- Comparisons to similar books
- No spoilers without a clear warning

### Editing or Deleting Your Reviews

1. Navigate to the book page or your profile's **My Reviews** section
2. Find the review you want to modify
3. Click the **Edit** (pencil icon) or **Delete** (trash icon) button
4. Make your changes and click **Save** or confirm deletion

## Managing Your Profile

### Viewing Your Profile

1. Click on your name or profile picture in the top-right corner
2. Select **My Profile** from the dropdown menu
3. Your profile displays your bio, reading stats, and recent activity

### Updating Your Profile

1. On your profile page, click **Edit Profile**
2. Update your name, bio, or profile picture
3. Click **Save Changes** when you're finished

### Managing Favorite Books

1. To add a book to favorites, click the heart icon on any book detail page
2. To view your favorite books, go to your profile and select the **Favorites** tab
3. To remove a book from favorites, click the heart icon again to unfavorite

## Getting Personalized Recommendations

### How Recommendations Work

BookReview Platform generates personalized book recommendations based on:
- Books you've rated and reviewed
- Books you've added to favorites
- Similar users' preferences
- Advanced AI algorithms that analyze reading patterns

### Finding Your Recommendations

1. Visit the **Home** page while logged in
2. Scroll to the **Recommended for You** section
3. Hover over any book to see why it was recommended
4. Click **Refresh Recommendations** to get new suggestions

### Improving Your Recommendations

The more you interact with the platform, the better your recommendations will be:
- Rate and review more books
- Add books to your favorites
- Update your reading preferences in your profile settings

## Troubleshooting Common Issues

### Login Problems

If you can't log in:
1. Check that you're using the correct email address
2. Use the **Forgot Password** link if you're unsure of your password
3. Clear your browser cookies and try again
4. Ensure your account is verified (check your email)

### Missing Reviews

If you can't find a review you wrote:
1. Check your profile's **My Reviews** section
2. Sort the book's reviews by "Your Review First"
3. Use the search function with your username

### Contact Support

If you need additional help:
1. Click on **Help** in the footer
2. Select **Contact Support**
3. Fill out the form with details about your issue
4. A support representative will respond within 24 hours
