## Getting Started

### Welcome to BookReview

BookReview is a platform designed for book lovers to discover, track, review, and discuss books. Our platform helps you:

- Discover new books based on your preferences
- Keep track of books you've read and want to read
- Share your thoughts through detailed reviews
- Connect with other readers and follow their recommendations
- Get personalized book recommendations

### System Requirements

The BookReview platform is accessible through:

- **Web Browsers**: Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge (latest versions)
- **Mobile Devices**: iOS 13+ and Android 8.0+
- **Screen Reader Compatible**: Works with JAWS, NVDA, and VoiceOver

### Registration

To create a new account:

1. Click the "Sign Up" button in the top right corner
2. Choose to register with your email or use social login (Google, Facebook)
3. If using email, enter your details and create a password
4. Verify your email address by clicking the link sent to your inbox
5. Complete your profile information

### Logging In

To log into your account:

1. Click the "Log In" button in the top right corner
2. Enter your email/username and password, or use social login
3. Check "Remember me" to stay logged in on trusted devices
4. If you forgot your password, click "Forgot Password?" to reset it

## Account Management

### Your Profile

Your profile is your presence on BookReview. To set up your profile:

1. Click your avatar in the top right corner
2. Select "Profile" from the dropdown menu
3. Click "Edit Profile" to update your information
4. You can customize:
   - Profile picture
   - Display name
   - Bio
   - Reading preferences
   - Privacy settings

### Privacy Settings

Control who sees your activity:

1. Go to your profile
2. Click "Settings" 
3. Select "Privacy"
4. Adjust settings for:
   - Who can see your reviews
   - Who can see your reading activity
   - Who can follow you
   - Email and notification preferences

### Managing Linked Accounts

To connect or manage social accounts:

1. Go to "Settings"
2. Select "Connected Accounts"
3. Link additional accounts or remove existing connections

### Subscription Management

If you're a premium subscriber:

1. Go to "Settings"
2. Select "Subscription"
3. View your current plan, billing information, and renewal date
4. Upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your subscription

## Finding Books

### Search Features

The BookReview search is powerful and flexible:

1. Click the search bar at the top of any page
2. Enter keywords, author names, titles, or ISBN numbers
3. Use advanced filters to refine results:
   - Genre
   - Publication year
   - Average rating
   - Length (page count)

**Search Tips:**
- Use quotes for exact phrases: `"Pride and Prejudice"`
- Exclude terms with minus: `fantasy -young adult`
- Search by author with @: `@"Jane Austen"`

### Browse Categories

Explore books by category:

1. Click "Browse" in the main navigation
2. Select a main category (Fiction, Non-Fiction, etc.)
3. Choose subcategories to narrow down your search
4. Sort results by popularity, publication date, or rating

### Recommendation Feeds

Your home feed shows personalized recommendations:

1. "For You" - Books tailored to your preferences and history
2. "Trending" - Popular books among readers with similar tastes
3. "New Releases" - Recent publications in your preferred genres
4. "Staff Picks" - Curated selections by BookReview editors

## Reading and Managing Your Books

### Your Library

Your library organizes your reading activity:

1. Access your library from the main menu
2. View books organized by shelves:
   - Currently Reading
   - Want to Read
   - Read
   - Custom shelves you create

### Adding Books to Your Library

To add a book:

1. Find the book you want to add
2. Click the "+" button or "Want to Read" button
3. Select the appropriate shelf
4. Optionally add a private note about the book

### Creating Custom Shelves

Organize books your way:

1. In your library, click "Create New Shelf"
2. Name your shelf (e.g., "Summer Reading," "Book Club Picks")
3. Choose privacy settings (public or private)
4. Add books by dragging them to the shelf or selecting them from your library

### Reading Progress

Track your reading journey:

1. For books you're currently reading, click "Update Progress"
2. Enter the page number or percentage completed
3. Optionally add notes about your current thoughts
4. View your reading statistics in the "Insights" section

## Writing and Managing Reviews

### Writing a Review

Share your thoughts on books you've read:

1. Navigate to the book's page
2. Click "Write a Review"
3. Rate the book (1-5 stars)
4. Write your review in the text editor
   - Use formatting tools for headings, bullet points, etc.
   - Flag any spoilers using the spoiler tag
5. Add optional tags to categorize your review
6. Click "Publish" or "Save as Draft"

### Review Guidelines

Quality reviews help the community. Great reviews:

- Express your honest opinion
- Provide specific examples
- Avoid excessive plot summary
- Mark spoilers appropriately
- Consider both strengths and weaknesses
- Stay respectful and constructive

### Managing Your Reviews

To edit or update reviews:

1. Go to your profile
2. Click the "Reviews" tab
3. Find the review you want to edit
4. Click "Edit" to make changes or "Delete" to remove it

### Interacting with Other Reviews

Engage with the community:

1. Like reviews that you find helpful
2. Comment to start a discussion
3. Share reviews on social media
4. Flag inappropriate content for moderator review

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

**Problem**: Can't log in
- Ensure your email and password are correct
- Check if Caps Lock is on
- Try resetting your password
- Clear your browser cookies and cache

**Problem**: Books not showing in your library
- Refresh the page
- Check your internet connection
- Verify you're logged into the correct account
- Make sure filters aren't hiding certain books

**Problem**: Can't post a review
- Ensure you're logged in
- Check that your review meets the minimum length requirement (50 characters)
- Verify you haven't already reviewed the book (one review per book)

### Contacting Support

## FAQ

### Account Questions

**Q: Can I change my username?**
A: Yes. Go to "Settings" > "Profile" and edit your username. Note that you can only change it once every 30 days.

**Q: How do I delete my account?**
A: Go to "Settings" > "Account" > "Delete Account". Please note that this action is permanent and will remove all your reviews and reading data.

### Reading and Reviews

**Q: What's the difference between rating and reviewing?**
A: Rating is simply assigning a star value (1-5), while a review includes your written thoughts about the book.

**Q: Can I import my reading history from other platforms?**
A: Yes. Go to "Settings" > "Import Data" and follow the instructions to import from Goodreads, LibraryThing, or StoryGraph.

### Technical Questions

**Q: Is my reading data private?**
A: By default, your reading activity is visible to other users, but you can adjust this in "Settings" > "Privacy".

**Q: Can I use BookReview offline?**
A: The mobile app has limited offline functionality. You can view previously loaded books and write reviews to sync when you're back online.

**Q: How do I export my data?**
A: Go to "Settings" > "Your Data" > "Export" to download a copy of your reading history, reviews, and notes.
